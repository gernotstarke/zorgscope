## Design Context

### Users

Gernot Starke is the owner, operator, and sole day-to-day user. Zorgscope is opened repeatedly for
very short sessions to answer one question: “does anything need me right now?” The primary job is to
scan GitHub work, site movement, and due tasks in seconds, recognize what changed, and then leave.
Repository readers and contributors are a secondary audience for the served documentation, not for
the operational dashboard itself.

### Brand Personality

Focused, dynamic, trustworthy. The interface should feel like a calm observatory for active systems:
alive enough to communicate change and ongoing work, but never anxious, noisy, or game-like. The
existing circular green logo combines charts, a trend line, and a telescope/keyhole motif; its lime
focus point is the natural accent and motion anchor.

### Aesthetic Direction

A compact technical instrument rather than a generic SaaS dashboard. Preserve the logo’s deep green,
lime, and charcoal identity while supporting both light and dark operating-system themes. Prefer
precise, state-driven movement, crisp hierarchy, and restrained asymmetry. Avoid neon cyberpunk,
decorative gradients, gratuitous card chrome, and constant attention-seeking animation. The frontend
remains server-rendered HTML with hand-written CSS and small progressive enhancements—no frontend
build step or heavy motion dependency.

### Design Principles

1. Optimize for a three-second glance: new, stale, failing, and in-progress states must read first.
2. Motion communicates state. Ambient motion is subtle; strong movement is reserved for refresh or
   newly changed data.
3. Make the lime focus point the signature accent instead of spreading accent color everywhere.
4. Keep animation GPU-friendly and compatible with the tight static-asset and cold-start budgets.
5. Support light/dark themes, never use color as the only signal, and provide a meaningful static
   alternative under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
